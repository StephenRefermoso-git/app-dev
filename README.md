# Bleach

> "If fate is a millstone, then we are the grist. Nothing can be done. So I wish for strength. If I cannot protect them from the wheel, then give me a strong blade." — Ichigo Kurosaki

## Overview
**Bleach** is a legendary anime and manga series created by Tite Kubo. It follows the adventures of **Ichigo Kurosaki**, a teenager who obtains the powers of a Soul Reaper (Shinigami)—essentially a death personification tasked with guiding souls to the Soul Society and fighting evil Hollows.

## Main Characters
* **Ichigo Kurosaki** – The orange-haired substitute Soul Reaper and primary protagonist.
* **Rukia Kuchiki** – The Soul Reaper who inadvertently shares her powers with Ichigo.
* **Uryu Ishida** – A Quincy, member of a clan of human archers historically at war with Soul Reapers.
* **Renji Abarai** – Lieutenant of the 6th Division and Ichigo's rival-turned-ally.

## Major Arcs
1. **Substitute Shinigami Arc** – Ichigo becomes a Soul Reaper and learns the ropes.
2. **Soul Society Arc** – Ichigo and friends infiltrate the spirit world to rescue Rukia.
3. **Arrancar / Hueco Mundo Arc** – A massive war against Sosuke Aizen's army of Hollow-human hybrids.
4. **Thousand-Year Blood War Arc** – The final epic conflict against the Quincy king, Yhwach.
